Mentor Terms of Service

Draft, written for the pre-incorporation stage — not legal advice. Worth a lawyer's pass once Batch Zero has paid mentors or larger cohorts.

1. Your role

As a Batch Zero mentor, you agree to advise founders in your assigned cohort on a volunteer basis — typically about 1–2 hours a week: one session with a team plus light prep. Unless you and Batch Zero agree otherwise in writing, mentoring is unpaid.

2. Use of your profile

You agree that Batch Zero may display your public LinkedIn profile information — name, photo, title, company, and a short bio — on the Batch Zero website and in related materials (the mentor roster, cohort announcements, social posts), to introduce you to founders and the community. You can ask us to update or remove this anytime by emailing hello@batchzero.co.

3. Confidentiality

Founders may share early-stage, non-public information about their startups with you during mentoring. You agree to keep that information confidential and not use it outside the context of advising them.

4. No ownership of founder work

Advice, feedback, or introductions you provide do not give you any ownership, equity, or intellectual property rights in a founder's company or work, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

5. Conduct

You agree to engage with founders professionally and respectfully, and to represent Batch Zero appropriately when acting as a mentor.

6. No liability

You're providing advice in good faith, as a volunteer. Neither you nor Batch Zero is liable for outcomes founders experience based on that advice. Batch Zero provides the mentoring program "as is," without warranties of any kind.

7. Ending the relationship

Either you or Batch Zero can end your involvement as a mentor at any time, for any reason, by notifying the other.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California.
