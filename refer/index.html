Know a founder? Get $100.

Send a high-school founder to Batch Zero. If their startup is accepted into a cohort, you get $100. Students, club leaders, ambassadors, teachers — anyone can refer. Cohort 01 applications close October 31.

//club leaders

Run a business, tech or entrepreneurship club?

Post your link in the club chat or put it on a slide at your next meeting. Every member whose startup is accepted earns you $100 — send two people, one gets in, that's $100 to you.

The rules
  • $100 per startup accepted into a Batch Zero cohort.
  • It goes to the person whose link they applied through. No link? To whoever they name under "Did someone send you here?"
  • One bonus per accepted startup, however many founders are on the team.
  • You can't refer yourself or your own team.
  • Paid within 14 days of the cohort starting, by Venmo, Zelle, PayPal or gift card. Under 18? We pay a parent or guardian, or send a gift card.
  • Ambassadors earn the same bonus on top of everything else.
