Mentors

Give a founder two months.

Two mentors per team, weekly sessions on the platform, eight weeks. We're looking for people who've built things and can say what they actually think. Five short steps, about fifteen minutes, and it saves as you go.

What we ask
  • 1–3 hours a week for 8 weeks
  • Sessions on the platform (recorded)
  • A short interview and a background check
  • Honest feedback, kindly delivered
