Who's in the room with you.

Two groups make Batch Zero work: mentors who have built things and give a team eight weeks, and student ambassadors who find the founders worth backing at their own schools.

One who knows your domain, one who has built a company end to end. Every mentor is interviewed and background-checked before being matched.

Student ambassadors

The people who find the founders.

Ambassadors run Batch Zero at their own school: they find students already building something, get them to apply, and bring their school into the first cohorts. It's the fastest way onto the founding team of something new.

01what you do

Find the builders

Every school has three or four students already shipping something. You know who they are, or you can find out in a week.

02what you get

A real title and a cut

Ambassador on the founding team, commission on what you bring in, and a reference from a program you helped build.

03what it takes

A couple of hours a week

Posters, a class announcement, a few conversations, one group chat. No experience needed — just knowing your school.
